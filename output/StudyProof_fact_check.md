**StudyProof draft: fact check**  
Checked 25 October 2026 against the pasted text, the local source at commit `c4401dd924f4eb1ee60656e5feeaaa2b847c6df5`, the event brief, and primary research/publisher sources.

The draft is substantially accurate about the implementation and appropriately describes educational benefits as untested. All three references are genuine and their bibliographic details match the sources checked. The main changes needed are a naming correction, more precise descriptions of the initial-response gate and cancellation, and clearer separation between research findings and the project's design rationale.

**Changes to make**

1. **Section 1: use the current application name.** Replace “Study Tracker brings…” with “StudyProof brings…”. `APP_NAME` is StudyProof in [the constants](C:/Users/ricky/Documents/GitHub/StudyProof/app/constants.py:4); the title and the rest of the draft already use that name.

2. **Abstract and section 2.2: qualify the initial-answer requirement.** The interface requires an initial non-empty message before hints and feedback, but does not establish that the message is a substantive answer. A local, in-memory check accepted “Please explain it to me.” as the stored first answer and unlocked a hint request without making a network call. This follows [submit_answer](C:/Users/ricky/Documents/GitHub/StudyProof/app/core/agent/coach_service.py:113) and [prepare_turn](C:/Users/ricky/Documents/GitHub/StudyProof/app/core/agent/coach_service.py:149). The later acknowledgement that first responses cannot prove unaided work is correct; add the gate's narrower meaning earlier too.

   Suggested replacement for the section 2.2 sentence: “Students select up to five notes or PDFs, receive a question, and submit an initial non-empty response before hints or feedback are unlocked. This encourages an initial attempt but does not verify its substance or whether it was unaided.”

   In the abstract, replace “attempt a question before receiving assistance” with “are prompted to attempt a question before requesting assistance”.

3. **Abstract and section 2.4: describe cancellation as application-side cancellation.** Cancelling sets a flag and prevents late results from being committed. The HTTP client checks that flag before and after its blocking request; it does not actively abort a request already running at the provider. Therefore “cancellable model requests” could imply more than is implemented. See [cancel_turn](C:/Users/ricky/Documents/GitHub/StudyProof/app/core/agent/coach_service.py:364) and [the HTTP request](C:/Users/ricky/Documents/GitHub/StudyProof/app/core/agent/provider_openai.py:50).

   Suggested wording: “The prototype supports cancellation of coaching turns and discards late replies; an in-flight provider request may continue.”

4. **Section 1: make the practice-testing claim match reference [2] more directly.** Dunlosky et al. support practice testing as an effective learning technique, rating it more highly than rereading. The review does not validate StudyProof's generated questions or private scores as diagnostic measures of understanding. The current sentence is defensible, but this version is closer to the evidence: “Practice testing generally supports retention more effectively than rereading alone [2].” See [the review](https://journals.sagepub.com/stoken/rbtfl/Z10jaVH/60XQM/full).

5. **Section 1: give the AI-fluency rationale its own support, or frame it as a risk.** The sentence about fluent AI explanations creating familiarity is plausible, but references [1] and [2] predate contemporary generative AI and do not directly establish this specific mechanism. Suggested wording: “A fluent AI explanation may feel understandable without showing that the learner can independently explain or apply the concept.” A relevant additional primary study is [the 2023 physics-education study of students' judgments of ChatGPT responses](https://journals.aps.org/prper/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevPhysEducRes.19.010142). It examines perceived accuracy and linguistic quality; it should not be presented as a direct test of StudyProof or proof that all fluent explanations cause an illusion of understanding.

6. **Section 3.2: distinguish UNESCO guidance from your engineering proposals.** Reference [3] supports privacy protection, human agency, equity and validation of educational AI. It should not be read as the source of a specific checklist for JSON credential storage, HTTPS enforcement or this application's transfer preview. Suggested opening: “UNESCO emphasises privacy protection and institutional validation of educational AI [3]. For this prototype, proposed controls include explicit transfer consent, a data preview, secure credential storage, HTTPS enforcement for remote services and provider-retention guidance.” See [UNESCO's publication](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/guidance-generative-ai-education-and-research).

7. **Author line: complete or remove the placeholders.** “[Additional author names], [Affiliation(s)], [Email(s)]” remains unfinished. The stated author's identity, affiliation and email ownership were not independently verified; these require author confirmation.

**Verification limits and test results**

I ran 12 existing test modules covering persistence, stopwatch/countdown logic, coaching, weak topics, recaps, question tracking, response repair, statistics, XP, streaks and medals, using existing dependencies and isolated test data. Result: **213 passed, 2 failed, plus 13 passed subtests**. Both failures occurred in stopwatch tests when a real dialog tried to initialise Tcl/Tk and the bundled environment could not load `init.tcl`. They do not establish that the stopwatch's rounding calculation is wrong, but they prevent a clean-pass claim. The full GUI suite, standalone GUI smoke check and packaged build were not run. No live AI provider request was made.

Claims that no student evaluation has occurred need confirmation from the project authors; repository inspection cannot establish the absence of an unpublished study. The draft correctly avoids claiming demonstrated learning gains, validated mastery scores, reliable live-model feedback or a completed accessibility evaluation. Keep those qualifications.

The source application and submitted draft were left unchanged. This file records the fact check and suggested wording only.
